Security isn't a settings toggle here — it's the default shape of the data.

✓

Tenant isolation by design

Every company's data is scoped at the query layer. A cross-company read returns not found, never a permission error that confirms the record exists.

✓

Role-based access control

Cashiers ring up sales; they can't void yesterday's sales or edit stock counts. Every route enforces its own permission, checked mechanically in CI.

✓

Full audit trail

Every price override, void, refund, and approval is logged with who, when, and what changed — visible to owners, not just support.

✓

Exact money, always

Every currency field is stored as an integer in minor units. No floating-point rounding drift, ever, on any report or receipt.
